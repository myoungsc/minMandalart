miniManda turns the Mandalart goal-setting method into a light 3x3 board you can start in a minute.

Put your core goal in the center and write up to eight sub-goals around it. A traditional Mandalart has 81 cells, but nine are all you need to begin.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Create as many boards as you like, one for each goal
• Add a memo and an action checklist to every sub-goal
• See progress update automatically as you check items off
• Mark a sub-goal as done when it has no checklist
• Share your board as a square image
• Use light or dark theme, or follow your device

MADE TO STAY SIMPLE
No sign-up is required. Your boards are saved on your device.
The layout also adapts to tablets and unfolded foldable phones, showing your board list and board side by side.

Available in English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, French, and Russian.
