2. Purpose and target audience
miniManda is a simple goal-planning app based on the Mandalart method: one core goal in the center, surrounded by 8 sub-goals. A traditional Mandalart has 81 cells, which many people find overwhelming. miniManda reduces it to a single 3x3 board so anyone can start in a minute.
It is for students, working adults, and anyone who sets personal goals (study, health, career, habits). It helps users break one goal into 8 concrete parts, attach an action checklist to each part, and see their progress at a glance.

3. How to use the main features (no login or sample files needed)
- Launch the app and tap "Create my first Mandalart" (or the + button when boards already exist).
- Enter a core goal (e.g. "Improve my spoken English") and tap "Create".
- On the board, tap an empty cell to add a sub-goal. In the sheet you can write a memo, mark the cell as done, or add checklist items. Progress is calculated from the checked items.
- Tap the center cell to edit the core goal.
- Use the board menu (top right) to share the board as a square image through the iOS share sheet, or to delete the board.
- Settings (gear icon on the home screen): Light / Dark / Follow system theme, "About Mandalart", and app version.
- On iPad, the board list and the selected board are shown side by side.
